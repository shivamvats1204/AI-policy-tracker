# Rationale note: AI policy activity across Indian states

**Sources.** I used only sources I could fetch within their robots.txt rules, with a 2-second per-host delay, a 5 MB response cap, raw snapshots and an audit log. The working core is seven Indian Express city RSS feeds (Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, Lucknow), its AI-section feed, Telangana Today's AI Mission tag page and DT Next Tamil Nadu. I also configured five Google News AI-policy queries and five official government listing pages. They did not work: Google News disallows the queries in robots.txt, Karnataka EITBT fails TLS validation, Tamil Nadu IT returns 403, and four other listings gave no links. I did not bypass any of this. The result is partial coverage, and the README says so.

**States.** Karnataka, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra and Uttar Pradesh: large states with dedicated IT or AI bodies and English-language local news, so sources were findable. This was a judgment call, not a ranking of policy activity. Events were found for Karnataka (1), Telangana (3) and Tamil Nadu (1), and none for Maharashtra or Uttar Pradesh. A toggle and `--all-states` flag lift the limit.

**Methodology.** The pipeline runs collect, extract, normalize, classify, deduplicate and validate. RSS items with AI signals but thin metadata are enriched from the article page, within a per-source budget. A state counts only when it is named in the headline, description or first paragraphs. Cities never imply a state; "UP", "Uttar Pradesh" and "Uttar-Pradesh" become one state; "MP" and "AP" count only in context. Dates are stored as ISO values with a precision flag, and ambiguous or missing dates stay unknown. The date is the publication date unless an event date has evidence.

**Where I drew the line.** An article becomes an event only if it mentions AI, names a state, shows a government signal and an action verb, and matches one of six categories (policy or mission, task force or CoE, MoU, government deployment, budget, regulation or ethics). I excluded private funding rounds, product launches without government involvement, negated launches, and interviews or progress reports with no milestone. Every excluded article is kept with its reasons. Deduplication merges only identical normalized headlines with the same state, category, status, day and numbers. Paraphrases stay separate for review.

**Presentation.** A Streamlit dashboard supports search, filters, provenance, exclusions and validation. A static page presents the same events with an "excluded" funnel, because 99% of collected articles are not AI policy events and I wanted that visible, not hidden. Everything is labelled as a candidate needing human review.
